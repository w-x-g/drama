# EP01《死局重启》提示词手册

> 覆盖：5 个角色资产 + 4 个场景资产 + 关键道具 + 26 镜首帧/运动提示
> 配套：[EP01-死局重启-制作包.md](EP01-死局重启-制作包.md)（分镜表、风险表、声音、剪辑）
> 用途：从零开始建资产、出首帧、跑图生视频，全部 prompt 可直接复制

---

## 本手册相对制作包的修正

| 问题 | 制作包第六节 | 本手册 |
|------|--------------|--------|
| 光线矛盾 | 固定后缀同时写了 `candle practical light` 和 `soft overcast morning light`，前世夜戏会自相矛盾 | 拆成 **三个后缀**：今生（晨光）/ 前世（烛光+冷月光）/ 闪回（褪色版） |
| 缺资产 prompt | 只有资产清单和命名，没有 prompt | 补齐 5 角色三视图 + 表情组、4 场景、6 道具的完整 prompt |
| 只有首帧 | 26 条 prompt 混合了首帧和运动描述 | 每镜拆成 **首帧 prompt** + **运动提示** 两段，分别喂给文生图和图生视频 |
| 文字三镜 | 只在表下写了一句「后期叠加」 | 给出 **空白纸替代 prompt + 后期叠字规格**（字体、颜色、混合模式） |
| 无 seed/参考图策略 | 「固定 seed」一句带过 | 给出每角色的 seed 基号与递增规则 |

## 一、怎么用这本手册

### 1.1 一条 prompt 的四段结构

```text
[景别 + 机位] + [主体 + 动作] + [环境 + 光线] + [风格后缀]
```

例：

```text
medium shot, eye level,                          ← 景别 + 机位
[SHEN16] pulls a branch of white pear blossoms   ← 主体 + 动作
out of a white porcelain vase, soft morning light, ← 环境 + 光线
[SUFFIX_A]                                       ← 风格后缀
```

**顺序不要乱。** 大多数模型对开头 15 个词的权重最高，景别和机位必须放在最前面，否则模型会自己决定构图。

### 1.2 占位符

本手册用五个占位符，**逐字替换**，不要改写、不要同义替换——一致性靠的就是逐字复现：

| 占位符 | 含义 | 完整锚点（见 2.3） |
|--------|------|---------------------|
| `[SHEN16]` | 沈岁宁 16 岁 | 定妆锚点 |
| `[SHEN23]` | 沈岁宁 23 岁（前世） | 定妆锚点 |
| `[JIANG]` | 江砚白 | 定妆锚点 |
| `[CHUNXING]` | 春杏 | 定妆锚点 |
| `[XIAOSI]` | 小厮 | 定妆锚点 |
| `[SUFFIX_A]` | 今生晨光后缀 | 见 1.3 |
| `[SUFFIX_B]` | 前世夜戏后缀 | 见 1.3 |
| `[SUFFIX_C]` | 闪回后缀 | 见 1.3 |

**拼装示例（S01-10 完整版）**：

```text
full shot, eye level, an ancient Chinese wooden door swings open,
a 25-year-old Chinese noble young man, clean handsome face, slightly downturned eyes, moon-white changshan, jade crown, stands in the doorway backlit by soft morning light, holding a branch of dew-covered white pear blossoms, wooden door frame forming a natural frame,
low saturation teal-grey palette, pear-blossom white, soft overcast morning light, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, fine film grain, low contrast, vertical 9:16, cinematic, no modern elements, muted restrained color grade
```

### 1.3 三个后缀（按场景选，不要混用）

**SUFFIX_A｜今生·晨光**（S01-06～18、22～26）

```text
low saturation teal-grey palette, pear-blossom white, soft overcast morning light, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, fine film grain, low contrast, vertical 9:16, cinematic, no modern elements, muted restrained color grade
```

**SUFFIX_B｜前世·夜**（S01-01～05）

```text
low saturation teal-grey palette, warm candle practical light, cold moonlight from the window, deep shadows, high contrast lighting, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, fine film grain, vertical 9:16, cinematic, no modern elements, muted restrained color grade
```

**SUFFIX_C｜闪回·前世但更褪色**（S01-19～21）

```text
low saturation teal-grey palette, warm candle practical light, cold moonlight from the window, deep shadows, high contrast lighting, slightly faded and desaturated, milky highlights, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, fine film grain, vertical 9:16, cinematic, no modern elements
```

> 闪回与前世只差一句 `slightly faded and desaturated, milky highlights`——观众不会明确察觉，但会「感觉」这段更远、更旧。

### 1.4 负面提示词

**全局负面**（每条都加）：

```text
deformed, bad anatomy, extra fingers, fused fingers, extra limbs, asymmetric eyes, crossed eyes, text, watermark, logo, subtitle, signature, blurry, low quality, oversaturated, plastic skin, CGI look, anime, 3d render, modern clothing, modern objects, jewelry, heavy makeup, teeth showing
```

**分镜追加负面**：

| 镜号 | 追加负面 | 原因 |
|------|----------|------|
| S01-04 | `eyes, upper face, nose` | 强制只出嘴部 |
| S01-06 | `two eyes, full face, bloodshot eyes, horror makeup` | 防恐怖谷 |
| S01-08 | `mirror frame edge artifacts, duplicated face` | 防镜像重影 |
| S01-20 | `readable text, calligraphy, characters, gibberish letters` | 强制空白纸 |
| S01-24 | `written characters, calligraphy, gibberish letters` | 强制空白纸 |
| S01-25 | `written characters, calligraphy, gibberish letters` | 强制空白纸 |
| S01-13 | `three people, duplicated body` | 防双人变多人 |
| S01-18 | `face visible, front view` | 强制背影 |

### 1.5 seed 与参考图

**seed 基号**（同角色所有镜头共用，按镜号递增避免完全重复）：

| 角色 | seed 基号 | 用法 |
|------|-----------|------|
| SHEN16 | `161600` | 定妆、表情组、所有含她的镜头 |
| SHEN23 | `232300` | 冷开场、闪回 |
| JIANG | `252500` | 所有含他的镜头 |
| CHUNXING | `151500` | S01-08 |
| XIAOSI | `151600` | S01-15 |
| 无人镜 | `040000` | S01-01、05、21、23、25（无脸，可自由抽） |

具体镜头的 seed = 基号 + 镜号后两位（如 S01-10 用 `252510`），抽卡失败时 +1 递增，**不要随机重置**。

**参考图顺序**（图生图 / 参考图模式）：

1. 角色定妆图（锁脸）
2. 场景定妆图（锁环境与光位）
3. 上一镜的尾帧（锁连续性）

### 1.6 首尾帧生成流程

```text
① 文生图出首帧（本手册第五节的「首帧」）
② 人工筛：脸、服装、构图、光线方向、手指
③ 首帧 → 图生视频，喂「运动提示」，时长按分镜表
④ 需要精确落点时，再补一张尾帧做「首尾帧插值」
⑤ 失败按第八节归因表修，不要盲目重抽
```

**时长与帧率**：24fps，3 秒 = 72 帧。2 秒的镜头（S01-06/22/23/25）建议生成 3 秒再裁前 2 秒，模型在前 0.5 秒通常还在「启动」。

## 二、角色资产 Prompt

> 资产图建议 **3:4 或 1:1**，不要用 9:16——竖屏会把三视图裁掉两侧。
> 三视图必须**同一张图**生成，分三次单独生成会导致三张脸。

### 2.3 定妆锚点（逐字复用，本节是唯一来源）

```text
[SHEN16]    a 16-year-old Chinese noble girl, oval face, almond eyes, pale skin, long black hair half-up with a simple silver hairpin, pale moon-white ruqun with light teal trim, no heavy makeup
[SHEN23]    a 23-year-old Chinese noble woman, same oval face and almond eyes, pale and thin, loose black hair, white inner robe
[JIANG]     a 25-year-old Chinese noble young man, clean handsome face, slightly downturned eyes, moon-white changshan, jade crown, gentle smile
[CHUNXING]  a 15-year-old Chinese maid, double buns, light green beijia over white blouse
[XIAOSI]    a 15-year-old Chinese servant boy, short dark green jacket, plain cloth belt, hair in a simple topknot
[PEIHENG]   a 20-year-old Chinese young general, sharp brows, calm eyes, dark blue riding coat over black armor, leather wrist guards, no helmet
```

### 2.1 沈岁宁 16 岁（SHEN16）｜本集第一主角

**① 三视图**

```text
character sheet, three views of the same person side by side: front view, 45-degree side view, back view, full body, neutral standing pose, arms relaxed, plain warm grey studio background, even soft lighting, no cast shadow,
a 16-year-old Chinese noble girl, oval face, almond eyes, pale skin, long black hair half-up with a simple silver hairpin, pale moon-white ruqun with light teal trim, no heavy makeup,
consistent character design, concept art, ultra detailed face, symmetrical, whole body in frame
```

负面：`cropped feet, cut off head, different faces across views, jewelry, elaborate embroidery`

**② 表情组（五张，同一 seed、同一机位，只换表情）**

固定前缀：

```text
portrait, head and shoulders, eye level, plain warm grey background, soft even lighting,
a 16-year-old Chinese noble girl, oval face, almond eyes, pale skin, long black hair half-up with a simple silver hairpin, pale moon-white ruqun with light teal trim, no heavy makeup,
```

| 表情 | 追加描述 |
|------|----------|
| 中性 | `neutral expression, calm eyes, lips relaxed` |
| 恍惚 | `dazed expression, unfocused eyes, lips slightly parted, as if just waking` |
| 冷 | `cold clear gaze, no warmth, lips pressed flat, chin slightly lowered` |
| 隐忍 | `restrained expression, jaw tight, eyes bright with unshed tears, brows slightly drawn` |
| 决绝 | `determined expression, steady gaze straight into camera, lips firmly closed` |

**③ 验收**：五张脸必须是同一个人；发髻、簪子、衣领样式完全一致；不允许出现耳环、步摇、浓妆。

### 2.2 沈岁宁 23 岁（SHEN23）｜前世

**① 三视图**

```text
character sheet, three views of the same person side by side: front view, 45-degree side view, back view, full body, neutral standing pose, plain warm grey studio background, even soft lighting,
a 23-year-old Chinese noble woman, same oval face and almond eyes, pale and thin, loose black hair, white inner robe,
sickly pallor, hollow cheeks, consistent character design, concept art, ultra detailed face
```

**② 表情组**（前缀同上，把人物换成 SHEN23）

| 表情 | 追加描述 |
|------|----------|
| 昏迷 | `eyes closed, face slack, shallow breathing, lips pale` |
| 病重 | `eyes half open, exhausted, feverish flush on cheeks` |
| 平静 | `eyes closed, calm, faint exhausted peace` |

**③ 验收**：与 SHEN16 **必须是同一张脸的不同年龄状态**——骨相一致，只是更瘦、更白、更疲。这是观众认出「前世今生是同一个人」的唯一依据。

### 2.4 江砚白（JIANG）

**① 三视图**

```text
character sheet, three views of the same person side by side: front view, 45-degree side view, back view, full body, neutral standing pose, plain warm grey studio background, even soft lighting,
a 25-year-old Chinese noble young man, clean handsome face, slightly downturned eyes, moon-white changshan, jade crown, gentle smile,
refined scholar appearance, consistent character design, concept art, ultra detailed face
```

**② 表情组（六个，本集最关键的一组）**

| 表情 | 追加描述 | 用在哪 |
|------|----------|--------|
| 温润笑 | `gentle warm smile, soft eyes, head slightly tilted` | S01-10/11 |
| 笑僵 | `smile still on the lips but frozen, eyes no longer smiling, jaw tight` | S01-13/14 |
| 慌张 | `eyes darting, brows raised, lips parted, barely contained panic` | S01-15 |
| 回避 | `eyes looking down and to the side, composed mouth, avoiding contact` | S01-16 |
| 微怔 | `caught off guard, lips slightly parted, blinking` | S01-17 |
| 平静 | `neutral composed expression, polite, unreadable` | S01-17 后景 |

**③ 验收（本集成败所在）**：

- 「温润笑」必须**真诚**，不能带一丝阴险。观众要觉得他是好人，S01-13 的「笑僵」才有杀伤力；
- 「笑僵」必须是**笑还在脸上、眼睛先死**，不是皱眉、不是嘴角下垂；
- 六个表情的玉冠、衣领、发型完全一致。

### 2.5 春杏（CHUNXING）

```text
character sheet, front view and 45-degree side view, full body, neutral pose, plain warm grey studio background, even soft lighting,
a 15-year-old Chinese maid, double buns, light green beijia over white blouse,
plain and neat, no jewelry, consistent character design, concept art
```

表情：`neutral`（中性）/ `curious, lips parted as if about to speak`（欲言又止）

### 2.6 小厮（XIAOSI）

```text
character sheet, front view and 45-degree side view, full body, plain warm grey studio background, even soft lighting,
a 15-year-old Chinese servant boy, short dark green jacket, plain cloth belt, hair in a simple topknot,
consistent character design, concept art
```

表情：`bowing slightly, head lowered`（躬身）/ `urgent, breathless`（急促）

### 2.7 裴珩（PEIHENG）｜EP01 不出图

本集只有马蹄声，**不生成任何画面**。此处只锁定文字锚点，EP02 建资产时直接复用，避免换脸。

## 五、26 镜逐镜 Prompt

> 每镜两段：**首帧**（喂文生图）+ **运动**（喂图生视频）。
> 首帧末尾已省略 `[SUFFIX_x]`，使用时请自行拼上，并加全局负面词。

## 六、文字三镜的后期方案

S01-20、S01-24、S01-25 一律**生成空白纸**，文字在剪辑软件里叠。

### 6.1 字幕层规格

| 项目 | 规格 |
|------|------|
| 字体 | 行楷（如「方正行楷」）或小楷，**不要用宋体/黑体** |
| 颜色 | 墨黑 `#1A1A1A`，不透明度 85–90% |
| 混合模式 | 正片叠底（Multiply），再叠 5% 的纸张噪点 |
| 透视 | S01-20 需做轻微透视变形，贴合纸面 |
| 动态 | S01-24 逐字写出（遮罩擦除，每字 0.25s）；S01-25 墨迹扩散用 AE 的 `Roughen Edges` + 缩放遮罩 |

### 6.2 叠字内容

**S01-20 信文**

> 温姑娘难产，命悬一线，我走不开。岁宁吉人自有天相，待我处置妥当便回。

排版：竖排从右往左，小楷，三行。**不要叠满**——只露中间两三行，其余虚化，避免观众读字分心。

**S01-24 / S01-25 落笔**

> 裴 珩

两个字，字号大，墨重。S01-24 写「裴」，S01-25 写「珩」并让末笔的墨晕开。

## 八、失败症状 → 归因 → 修法

| 症状 | 归因 | 修法 |
|------|------|------|
| 脸不一样 | 锚点被改写 / seed 变了 | 逐字复现锚点，恢复 seed |
| 服装细节漂移 | 锚点里没写全 | 把衣领/簪子/玉冠写进锚点，不要靠模型补 |
| 出现第三个人 | 双人 prompt 描述不清 | 加负面 `three people, duplicated body`，明确「only two people」 |
| 手指数错 | 手部特写 | 换成物件特写，或只拍手腕，或后期遮 |
| 中文糊字 | 文字戏 | 改空白纸，后期叠字 |
| 画面太亮/太现代 | 后缀缺失 | 检查 SUFFIX 是否拼上 |
| 前世戏出现晨光 | 后缀混用 | 前世一律用 SUFFIX_B |
| 表情太凶（江砚白） | 「温柔」没写够 | 锚点加 `gentle, sincere, warm`，负面加 `sinister, smirking` |
| 镜头动了但内容没动 | 运动提示太抽象 | 写具体动作动词 + 方向，如 `raises her eyes to the left` |
| 推镜变形 | 推得太多 | 压到 10% 以内，或改固定机位后期裁切推 |
